## 🚀 Final Step: Deploy to Vercel (2 Minutes)

### **Option 1: Web Dashboard (EASIEST)** ⭐

**Step 1: Go to Vercel**
1. Open: https://vercel.com
2. Click "Sign Up" or "Log In"
3. Click "Continue with GitHub"
4. Authorize Vercel to access your GitHub

**Step 2: Import Your Project**
1. Click "Add New..." → "Project"
2. Click "Continue with GitHub"
3. Find `purva-portfolio` repository
4. Click "Import"

**Step 3: Configure**
- **Project Name:** purva-portfolio (auto-filled)
- **Framework:** Vite (auto-detected)
- **Root Directory:** ./ (default)
- Leave other settings as default

**Step 4: Deploy**
1. Click "Deploy"
2. Wait 2-3 minutes for build and deployment
3. See your live URL! 🎉

**Your live URL will be something like:**
```
purva-portfolio-abc123.vercel.app
```

---

### **Option 2: CLI (If you want to use terminal)**

**Step 1: Login to Vercel**
Open a terminal and run:

vercel login

cd c:\Users\DELL\Downloads\portfolio-template-main\portfolio-template-main

Wait for deployment to complete!
